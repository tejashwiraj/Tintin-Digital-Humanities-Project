Tintin, Modernity & the Absurd

A Digital Humanities Research Corpus

A Digital Humanities Research Project exploring modernity, travel, technology, objects and visual culture in The Adventures of Tintin.

RESEARCH QUESTIONS

1. How does Tintin represent modernity through movement, technology, objects and visual form?
2. How does Tintin represnt Hero's Journey, while exploring the absurdist and exsistalist elements throughout his journey?
3. How does machines and technology supports the rhythm of the movement, travel and modernirty of 20th century?
4. How is Tintin adapted in differnt media and forms that represents broader topography of popular culture and visual culture?
